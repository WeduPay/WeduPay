Why we’re here

Our Mission

To make online payments accessible to millions of consumers across Africa by connecting mobile wallets, merchants, and payment networks through a simple phone-number-based payment experience.

Where we’re going

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading mobile-wallet commerce network, enabling seamless payments between consumers and businesses regardless of wallet provider, bank, or country.

Good questions

Built around
your wallet.

Here’s what to know about WeduPay today.

Can I pay with WeduPay right now?

Not yet. WeduPay is in development and is not currently accepting or processing payments. Education payments are our first planned use case.

Do I need a bank card to use WeduPay?

The platform is being designed so consumers can pay participating merchants using a registered mobile number linked to a supported wallet, without needing a bank card. Wallet support will depend on the market at launch.

Which mobile wallets will be supported?

Our target payment methods include services such as EcoCash, InnBucks, Mukuru Wallet, OMari, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, Zamtel Kwacha, TNM Mpamba, M-Pesa, e-Mola, and Mkesh. These are planned integrations, not currently available payment options.

Why is education the first focus?

Tuition payments give WeduPay a focused first use case to prove the platform, build trust, and develop wallet integrations before expanding into broader merchant payments.
