The problem we’re working on

Mobile money is part of everyday life.
Online checkout often expects a card.

For students paying an international institution, the gap between a local mobile money balance and an online payment can mean extra steps and uncertainty.

01 □

Cards aren't always an option

Some students do not have access to an international debit or credit card accepted by an online institution.

02 ↗

Paying can take extra help

Cross-border tuition payments may require help from another person or a payment intermediary.

03 ◉

Local balances meet global checkouts

Money in a mobile wallet is not always directly usable for online or international transactions.

These are the problems WeduPay is designed to explore. We do not make market-size or adoption claims without verified evidence.

The bridge we’re building

A bridge between
mobile money and online payments.

WeduPay is developing the concept and exploring the partnerships and infrastructure needed to connect African mobile money with global digital payments, starting with tuition payments for students.

No international bank card required

Authorization through the provider

Payment approval is intended to happen through the customer’s selected provider or an authorized processing partner.

Where future integrations support it, customers should be able to review the amount, currency and applicable fees before confirming.

    Choose an available and supported mobile money provider.

  3. 03✓

    Review and authorize

    Review the amount, currency and applicable fees, then approve through the provider’s authorized process.

  4. 04↗

    Receive payment confirmation

    A provider or licensed processing partner confirms status for the student and institution.

WeduPay does not identify a wallet from a telephone number or network prefix. Provider selection and authorization must be supported by verified integrations.

Frequently asked questions

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What is WeduPay?

WeduPay is building a bridge between African mobile money and global digital payments, starting with education. The concept and required partnerships and infrastructure are still in development.

Do I need a Visa or Mastercard?

WeduPay is exploring a future payment experience using local mobile money rather than requiring an international bank card. No such payment service is currently available.

Does WeduPay hold my money?

WeduPay does not currently hold customer funds or operate a custodial wallet. Any future payment flow will depend on the appropriate licensed providers and partners.

Which mobile money providers will be supported?

Potential providers are still being explored. Names shown on this site are examples of target methods, not confirmed integrations or partnerships.

Is WeduPay operational?

No. WeduPay is under development. Payment services, provider integrations and merchant acceptance are not yet operational.

Can merchants and educational institutions join?

Yes, merchants and institutions can express interest through the early access waitlist. Submitting interest does not establish a partnership or service agreement.

When will early access be available?

A launch date has not been announced. Join the waitlist to express interest; it is not a guarantee of access or a payment service.
