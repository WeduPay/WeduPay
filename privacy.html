When this notice applies

This draft describes the proposed early-interest waitlist on the WeduPay website. WeduPay is an early-stage initiative. The waitlist is not a payment service, account, or guarantee of access. Do not send payment credentials, wallet PINs, passwords, identity documents, or financial account information through it.

Information requested

Depending on your selected interest, the form requests a name and email address, country, and role-specific information: for students, the phone number registered to their mobile wallet; for merchants, a business name and business type; for institutions, an institution name and the type of interest. The waitlist does not verify phone-number ownership or identify a wallet. The form also records the time consent is submitted. The server may temporarily process an IP address for abuse prevention and rate limiting; it is not intended to retain that address in the waitlist database.

Purposes and legal basis

The proposed purposes are to understand interest in the project, respond to inquiries, and contact people who consent to receive early-access updates. The final notice must state the applicable legal bases under the laws governing each target country. Consent can be withdrawn by contacting [privacy contact]; the process and effect of withdrawal must be confirmed before launch.

Storage and service providers

The proposed implementation uses Supabase for waitlist storage and Upstash Redis for short-lived rate-limiting counters. These services are not represented as configured or active. Before collection begins, WeduPay must identify the actual providers and data locations, review processor terms, configure access controls, and disclose any cross-border transfers and safeguards required by law.

Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information, and to complain to a regulator. Security and changes

The proposed design keeps database credentials on the server, restricts database access, and uses rate limiting. No online service can promise absolute security. This notice must be updated to reflect actual practices before collection and whenever those practices change.
